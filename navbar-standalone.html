00The Standard

A guide rates the kitchen whether or not the kitchen agrees to be rated.

We evaluate whoever we choose, with or without authorization, and publish whatever we find. No invitation required. No quiet exits. No machine judges.

01Current project

“Hallucination” — for briefs and contracts.

One known error introduced into a real document, a single fixed review instruction, and a binary check of whether the tool returns a corrected document.

02Methodology

The citation-integrity benchmark.

Errors are introduced and diffed in code; the pass/fail check is binary and confirmed by a person — never an LLM grading another model's output.

03Take part

The board grows by contribution.

Send us a brief, a contract, a known hallucination, a tool to test, or a result to verify.
